Invaded

Mixed Media· 2023

Invaded explores the fragile boundary between art, engineering, and technology — asking whether integration with machines leads to enhancement or alienation. The piece combines two contrasting materials: a traditional sculptural form carved from aerated concrete and discarded circuit components collected from laboratory waste. Cables and circuits spread across the surface like living ivy, bugs, and mushrooms, suggesting that technology — once external — now grows inward, becoming part of the body’s ecosystem. What emerges is a system both parasitic and symbiotic, questioning whether technological integration signifies evolution, dependency, or the rise of a new kind of nature.
