ZEYNEP BEGÜM KARA

Hello! I’m Begüm — a Master’s student in Computer Science at Koç University, focusing on Human–Computer Interaction. I received my Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science from Bilkent University.

My research interests lie in the intersection of art, technology, design, and engineering — exploring how these domains converge to shape the ways we create, think, and connect.

My current work focuses on human–AI collaboration in creative fields, intelligent user interfaces, and multimodal interaction. My core research aim is to design intelligent systems that bring out the best in both humans and AI — amplifying one another through collaboration and fostering meaningful, human-centered interaction.

Always open to collaborations and conversations. Feel free to reach out!
